The Vulture

A volcano, two lakes, one wine.

Northern Basilicata is a land to explore slowly. Here are a few ideas for a day in the Vulture, with a stop at the table.

The Vulture area

Before lunch, the Vulture.

Taverna Paradiso is a good starting point, or finishing line, for exploring northern Basilicata.

Monte Vulture

The sleeping volcano

Rionero stands on the slopes of Monte Vulture, an ancient extinct volcano. Its volcanic soils give character to the area's wine and produce.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

Monticchio lakes

Two lakes in the crater

Lago Grande and Lago Piccolo fill the volcano's crater, among woods and the Abbey of San Michele. Many guests come to the table after a walk here.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

Rionero in Vulture

Giustino Fortunato's town

In the centre, the 18th-century Palazzo Giustino Fortunato houses the town library and honours the great southern thinker born here in 1848.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

Local produce

From the woods to the table

Chestnuts, cured meats, cheeses, crusco peppers and the Vulture's mineral waters: Lucanian food culture grows from these.

Illustrative image · Unsplash

A day in the Vulture

Ideas for your visit.

  1. 01 · 16 km

    Morning at the Monticchio lakes

    A walk around Lago Grande and Lago Piccolo, inside the volcano's crater, and a stop at the Abbey of San Michele.

  2. 02 · 12:30

    Lunch at the taverna

    Back to Rionero for a Lucanian lunch: the house starter, handmade pasta and a glass of Aglianico.

  3. 03 · Rionero

    Afternoon in the centre

    Palazzo Giustino Fortunato, home of the town library and the Aglianico del Vulture consortium, and the town's churches.

  4. 04 · Melfi · Venosa

    Among the villages

    Melfi and Venosa are a short drive away: castles, history and Aglianico vineyards.

Sources: FAI, Wikipedia, DOC/DOCG regulations. Distances are approximate.

08Aglianico del Vulture

The wine of the volcano.

Rionero is one of the towns of Aglianico del Vulture, Basilicata's signature red, and home to its protection consortium. A ruby-coloured wine, intensely aromatic and full-bodied, that softens with age.

At the taverna, guests talk about the house Aglianico, suggested as a pairing. The wine list will be published together with the restaurant.
