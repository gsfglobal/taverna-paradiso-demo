Our cuisine

The specialities of the taverna.

Local starters, handmade pasta, meat and salt cod: everyday Lucanian cooking, the kind guests come back for.

The pasta ritual

Three steps, no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Flour and water

    Strascinati start from a simple dough, worked by hand.

  2. 02

    The gesture

    The pasta is dragged across the board with the fingertips: hence the name.

  3. 03

    The crusco

    The dried pepper is fried for a few seconds and crumbled over the dish.
